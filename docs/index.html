One pathogen genome in, every next step out

When a new virus appears, someone has to decide which of its proteins a drug should aim at. The evidence for that decision is thinnest when the virus is newest. This system makes the decision from the genome's database ID alone, and records how it got there at every step.

In plain terms

A new virus turns up. Which of its proteins should a drug aim at? A person usually answers that question, from the literature and from viruses already understood.

What goes in

One accession number, the public database ID for a genome. Nothing else: no target name, no shortlist, no structure.

What runs

Five steps work out which proteins the virus makes, rank them as drug targets, predict one protein's 3-D shape, and check whether a drug would have anywhere on it to grip. No person names the target at any point.

Nobody has solved a structure for a brand-new virus, so a predicted one is all there is to measure.

What comes out

A ranked shortlist of the proteins a drug could be aimed at. Each score is broken into its parts, and each surface is measured, predicted, or refused with a reason.

The test below ran on SARS-CoV-2, with SARS-CoV-2 itself hidden from every database the system could reach, so it could not recognise the answer. It still put the real drug targets at the top, including the one an approved antiviral hits.

How a relative is matched, and what happens when there is nonetwo tiers, then a fallback
  • A close relative, in milliseconds. Found from runs of five residues that the two sequences share. This is what the match column reports.
  • A fainter resemblance, more slowly. A second pass groups residues by chemical type, and reports a score of its own instead.
  • No relative at either tier. The protein is classed by how water-repelling its residues are. That returns a class rather than a name, and cannot outrank a real match.

That is the one chain the record reports in full. Below is every chain this step was handed, with where its sequence was found in this genome and where it fell against the ceiling.

  • A chain over the ceiling cannot be folded whole. That does not put the target out of reach.
  • A drug is rarely aimed at a whole chain. It is aimed at a pocket or a catalytic site inside one, and the relative's entry already says where those are.
  • So the length test is applied to each unit, and not to the chain that holds them. Asked in the other order — is this chain 400 residues or fewer? — one answer decides a whole protein, and every region inside a long one is discarded along with it. That is what put 8 of the 12 located chains beyond reach: not the hard ones, the long ones. The regions are therefore read off the relative first and measured against the ceiling one at a time afterwards, so a target is refused only when nothing inside it fits either.

Why the predicted group is kept apart, when the sum is the same arithmetic4 notes
  • A deposited structure is something that happened. A protein was purified, crystallised or frozen, put in a beam, and coordinates were fitted to what came back.
  • A predicted structure is a claim a model makes from the sequence, with nobody having looked at the molecule.
  • Only the deposited patch comes with a spread. The same chain has been solved several times and the structures disagree. A prediction returns the same coordinates for the same sequence, and carries a confidence per residue instead, 62.6 out of 100 for the main protease.
  • A predicted chain is folded alone, with no partner and no membrane around it, so faces that are buried in life sit out in the open and the patch inflates. That is the likeliest reason the main protease's predicted patch comes out several times larger than its deposited one. It cannot be separated from ordinary prediction error, so the record states the ratio and names no cause.
  • The gate is a statement about this class of molecule, not about the target, so it never becomes a term in the ranking.
Why a predicted surface earns its place at all2 notes
  • A deposited structure exists only where somebody already did the work, which tracks attention rather than importance. The two enzymes approved antivirals hit have several structures apiece, while the membrane and accessory proteins have none anywhere.
  • A pipeline that can only measure deposited structures answers best on the targets that need it least, and says nothing at all about a genuinely new pathogen, which is the case this project exists for.
  • A prediction does not replace a measurement. It replaces no answer with an answer that states where it came from.

  • Everything above measures the largest water-repelling patch anywhere on a structure. That answers whether the protein could hold a molecule of this class at all.
  • The next question is where on the protein to put one. The curators have already marked the residues that do the work, the catalytic and ligand-binding ones, so the same arithmetic can be run there instead of at the chain's best patch.
1 · mark Take the residues the curated entry marks as doing the work.
2 · reach Collect everything within 13.8 Å of them. A macrocycle covers about 600 Å² when it binds, which makes it roughly that far across.
3 · intersect Keep the part that is also in the patch step 4 found. 13 of 23 folded units have at least one marked residue inside the coordinates. The rest state which of four absences applies.
  • Step 4's question has a yes-or-no answer: does the unit's best patch reach the marked residues at all? 12 of 13 units say yes, which is too coarse to act on.
  • The share of the patch that sits at those residues says how much. Y1 on Papain-like protease nsp3 holds 94 % of its unit's patch there, and AP3A_BC279 Protein 3 holds 19 %, so most of the surface that second unit was scored on is somewhere else. Both count as a yes.
Not every marked residue is a place to put a binderwhy two figures are reported
  • Most plain site annotations on one of these long proteins mark where an enzyme cuts the chain. A cut point sits at the end of a finished chain by construction, so a neighbourhood drawn around one is a neighbourhood of a loose end. Both measurements are therefore reported.
